About Which Cabin

Which Cabin is an independent guide to choosing a cabin on the biggest cruise ships. Most cruise sites tell you about the ship; we answer one question: which cabin and deck should you ask for, and which should you avoid?

What we cover

25 ships from Royal Caribbean, Carnival Cruise Line, Norwegian Cruise Line, MSC Cruises, Celebrity Cruises, Princess Cruises, Disney Cruise Line. We add ships when we can check their layout against the line's own deck plan.

How we work

Every ship page starts from the deck-by-deck layout of the current deck plan, which we turn into an original deck noise map. Ship facts come from cited sources. We write every tip ourselves and leave out anything we cannot verify. See sources and methods.

Independence

Which Cabin is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any cruise line. Ship and line names are used only to identify the ships we describe.

Spotted something out of date after a refit? Tell us and we will re-check it.
