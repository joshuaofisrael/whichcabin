How it works

For every ship we take the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan, mark each deck as cabins, public venues or a mix, and flag cabin decks that sit directly under pools and sports areas or over bars and theatres. Frequently asked questions

Which cabin should I book on a cruise?

Choose the deck first, then the spot on the deck, then the room type. A cabin on a deck with cabins above and below, midship and away from elevators, beats a fancier room under the pool deck.

Which deck is the quietest on a cruise ship?

The quietest cabin decks have other cabin decks directly above and below. Each ship page maps those decks, and the quietest-decks guide lists them for every ship we cover.

Which cabin is best if I get seasick?

Book a midship cabin on one of the lowest passenger decks, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
