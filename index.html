How it works

For every ship we take the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan, mark each deck as cabins, public venues or a mix, and flag cabin decks that sit directly under pools and sports areas or over bars and theatres. Then we add ship-specific tips and cite every source. We are independent: no cruise line pays for coverage. Sources and methods.
