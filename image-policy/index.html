Image policy

Last updated 15 Nov 2026.

Diagrams

Deck noise maps and charts on Which Cabin are original diagrams generated by us from cited facts. They are not copies of any cruise line's deck plan artwork.

Photos

The site currently uses no ship photos. We do not use cruise line photos or deck plan images without permission.

AI-generated images

If we add AI-generated illustrations, each will carry a visible "AI-generated image" credit, will not depict real people, and will never be presented as a photo of a real ship or cabin.

Logos and trademarks

We do not display cruise line logos. Names are used only to identify ships.

Questions

If you believe an image on this site uses your work, see our copyright policy or contact us.
