Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Which Cabin (whichcabin.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

On whichcabin.com we use Cloudflare Web Analytics and, if you accept, Google Analytics 4.

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views. It does not use cookies or local storage and does not track you across sites. It records basic, aggregated information such as the page visited, referrer, browser type and country. The launch site includes this beacon on every page.

Google Analytics 4 measures how the site is used. It does not load until you press Accept on the cookie banner, and only when a measurement id is set for the launch build. Before that choice we set Google Consent Mode v2 defaults to denied, including analytics storage, ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation. If you accept, Google Analytics 4 may set cookies to recognise this browser. If you reject, we do not load it. Ad storage stays denied either way, because the site shows no ads. The Cookie settings link in the footer opens the banner again. We remember the choice in local storage on your device, not in a cookie of our own.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service, so we can reply. We use them only to respond and keep them only as long as needed.

Advertising

The site shows no ads today. If we add ads in future, for example through Google AdSense, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You will be able to opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info/choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we will ask for consent through a certified consent management platform before any advertising cookies are set. We will update this policy before any ads go live.

Cookies

Which Cabin does not set its own cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies. Google Analytics 4 may set cookies only after you accept. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. The site works if you reject analytics. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your mind, or clear this site's data in your browser to forget the saved choice.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security and operations.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you via the contact form. Residents of some US states and other countries may have additional rights, which we will honour.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will post any changes here with a new date.
