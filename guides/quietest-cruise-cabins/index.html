How we worked it out

For each ship we took the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan and marked each deck as cabins, public venues or a mix. A cabin deck counts as quiet when the decks directly above and below are also cabin decks. It is a deck-level shortcut: a venue may only fill part of a deck, so a cabin at the far end can still be quiet. Details on our sources and methods page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the quietest cabin on a cruise ship?

A midship cabin on a deck with cabin decks directly above and below, a few doors from the elevators and not next to a connecting door or service area.

Are higher decks quieter on a cruise ship?

Not automatically. The top cabin deck is often directly under the pool or sports deck. The quietest decks are usually in the middle of the ship's cabin block.
