Cruise ship cabins to avoid

Short answer: avoid cabins directly under a pool, sports or buffet deck, directly over a theatre, casino or promenade, low and forward near the thrusters, beside elevators or service doors, and any category labelled partial or obstructed view.

  1. Under the pool or Lido deck. Deck chairs, cleaning and set-up start early. Every ship page lists the deck that sits directly under the pools.
  2. Over the theatre, casino, nightclub or promenade. These stay busy late. Bass carries upward through the structure.
  3. Under the running track or sports court. Footsteps and ball games overhead.
  4. Low and forward. Closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when docking, and forward cabins feel the most motion.
  5. Next to elevators, stair landings and crew doors. Foot traffic and doors banging. Unlabelled blocks on a deck plan are usually service areas.
  6. Connecting cabins if you are not travelling with the people next door: the connecting door lets more sound through than a wall. See who connecting cabins suit.
  7. Partial or obstructed view. Usually lifeboats or structure in the way. Lines label these, so read the category name.
  8. Inward-facing or promenade-view balconies if you want a sea view. They overlook an inside neighbourhood or promenade instead.

Frequently asked questions

Which cabin is the worst on a cruise ship?

For most people, a cabin directly under the pool deck or directly above a nightclub or theatre, because of noise. For people prone to seasickness, a low forward cabin.

Are cabins near the elevator bad?

They are convenient but get more foot traffic and noise. A few doors away from the elevator lobby gives you most of the convenience with less noise.

What does obstructed view mean on a cruise?

Something, usually a lifeboat or part of the ship's structure, blocks some of the window or balcony view. Lines price and label these cabins separately.
