Which cruise cabin sleeps a family of five?

Short answer: a family of five fits in one cabin only where the line prints an occupancy of 5 or more. Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Norwegian, MSC and Disney each publish at least one such room. Celebrity's pages checked the same day do not label any single stateroom as sleeping 5. The word "family" in a room name is not the occupancy.

Checked 21 Nov 2026 against each line's own stateroom or deck-plan page. No prices and no sailings. A quiet deck is a separate question, answered on the quietest-decks table and on each ship page.

Read "up to" as a ceiling

Lines publish occupancy in three different ways, and mixing them up is how a party of five gets a room that sleeps four. A fleet page that says "up to 6" is naming the largest room in that group. A deck plan that says "up to 4" on one name and "up to 6" on the next name is the split that matters. A connecting pair is two cabins, not one. Where a page does not print a number, this guide does not invent one.

Royal Caribbean

Royal Caribbean's FAQ on staterooms for five or more, checked 21 Nov 2026, says the line offers several staterooms with an occupancy of 5 or more, that those rooms are typically larger and may have extra beds or sofa beds, and that they are not available to book on the website. The rooms page is the ceiling. Cruise rooms, checked the same day, lists interior staterooms at up to 340 sq ft and up to 6 guests, the ocean-view block at up to 354 sq ft and up to 8, the balcony block at up to 298 sq ft and up to 8, and the suite block at up to 2,766 sq ft and up to 9. The same page names Spacious Interiors, connecting rooms, Ultra Spacious Ocean View rooms, and the Ultimate Family Suite. It places the Ultimate Family Treehouse, a three-deck suite, on Hero of the Seas. Hero is not one of the ships on this site.

The ship page is where the ceiling breaks into real room names. On the Independence of the Seas deck plan for the view dated 8 March 2026 to 12 April 2026, a standard Ocean View is up to 4 guests at 162 sq ft. The Ultra Spacious Ocean View is up to 6, at 293 sq ft, with two Royal King beds that each convert to twins and two fold-down Pullman beds. The Spacious Interior is also up to 6, at 340 sq ft, with the same bed note. The deck-plan key includes a mark for "Stateroom with occupancy 5 and up", separate from the marks for up to 4 and up to 3. So on Independence of the Seas a family of five is in one of those high-occupancy names, not in an ordinary ocean view. The rooms-page pictures also name Wonder of the Seas, Utopia of the Seas and Symphony of the Seas, but those pictures are not a per-cabin occupancy list. Use the ship's own deck plan for the number.

Carnival

Carnival's stateroom overview, checked 21 Nov 2026, lists "Sleeps up to 5 guests" as an available feature of interior, ocean view, balcony and suite rooms, and it says each of those four types is available on all ships. Further down, the same page says some staterooms can be configured in different ways and some sleep up to five, and that Deluxe Ocean View staterooms sleep up to five and come with an extra half-bath. That is a ceiling plus one named type. It is not a claim that every interior on every ship sleeps five.

Family Harbor is a different sentence. The overview describes the lounge and the perks, and it names the ships, but it does not print an occupancy for Family Harbor itself. The ships it names that we cover are Carnival Celebration, Carnival Horizon, Carnival Jubilee, Mardi Gras, Carnival Panorama and Carnival Vista. Havana and Terrazza rooms on that page are restricted to guests 12 or older, which is an age rule, not a headcount. The Carnival hub is the place to compare decks once the room type is settled.

Norwegian

The cleanest Norwegian list is a single ship. Norwegian Joy's stateroom options, checked 21 Nov 2026, print a guest count on every category. For five or more: the Haven Family Villa Suite with Balcony (category HH) is up to 5 guests, 501 sq ft, deck 15. Family Oceanview (O4, O5) is up to 5, 240 to 372 sq ft, on decks 5 and 9 through 13. The Family Suite (SP) is up to 6, on decks 10, 11, 12, 14 and 15. Two Haven 2-bedroom family villas (H5 on deck 17, H6 on deck 18) are up to 6. The Haven Premier Owner's Suite (H2) is up to 8. The categories with "family" in the name that stop at 4 are the Family Club Balcony Suite, the Family Balcony and the Family Inside. A family of five does not fit those three. The page says images, square footage and configurations are representative only.

The fleet suites page, checked the same day, is less tidy, and the card at the top of a category sometimes disagrees with the paragraph under it. Where they disagree, this guide does not pick a winner. Family Suites: the card says sleeps up to 6, and the paragraph says sleeping space for up to six. That one matches. The page lists them on Norwegian Aqua, Norwegian Prima and Norwegian Viva, among ships we do not cover. Family Suites with one or two bedrooms: the card says sleeps up to 6 at 880 sq ft, and the paragraph says up to 650 sq ft and up to eight guests, with an asterisk that capacity varies by ship. Both numbers are at least 5, and the page names the same three ships. Forward-facing suites and penthouses: the card and the paragraph both say up to five. Among ships we cover, the page names Norwegian Jewel. Aft-facing suites: the card says sleeps up to 5, and the paragraph says accommodating up to four. Aqua, Jewel, Prima and Viva are on that list. Because the page contradicts itself, an aft-facing suite is not treated here as a confirmed five-guest room. The 3-Bedroom Garden Villa card says sleeps up to 7, and the paragraph says up to eight. Jewel is the ship we cover on that list. Norwegian Joy is the page to open first if the party is five, because its categories do not argue with themselves.

MSC

MSC's idea of a family cabin is a pair, not a bigger single room. The cabin FAQ, checked 21 Nov 2026, says family cabins have two or more connecting cabins, each with its own bathroom, and can accommodate up to 10 people. The names it lists are Superfamily Plus, Superfamily with ocean view or balcony, Superfamily, Family balcony and Family ocean view. It says they are available with the Fantastica experience on MSC Virtuosa, Grandiosa, Bellissima, Meraviglia, Seaview, Seaside, Preziosa, Divina and Opera. Of those, this site has MSC Virtuosa, MSC Grandiosa, MSC Meraviglia, MSC Seaside and MSC Divina. MSC World America and MSC World Europa are not on that list. This page does not add them.

Seaside also publishes single rooms at five. Its cabins and suites page, checked 21 Nov 2026, says the Yacht Club Deluxe Suite can accommodate up to 5 (about 25 sq m, decks 16 to 18), the Grand Suite Aurea up to 5 (decks 9 to 13), the Premium Suite Aurea with whirlpool up to 5 (decks 9 to 15), the Premium Interior up to 5 (about 28 sq m, deck 10) and the Deluxe Interior up to 5 (14 sq m, decks 5 to 10). The Two-Bedroom Grand Suite Aurea is the one that goes to 6 (decks 11 to 14). Several other Seaside categories on that page, including the Yacht Club Royal Suite, do not print a guest count. Those are left out on purpose. The page spells "accomodate" and says the image is representative only.

Disney

Disney's clearest headcount is on the accessible staterooms page, checked 21 Nov 2026. These figures are for the accessible rooms the page names. They are not a claim about every standard cabin with the same category code. A party that does not need an accessible room still has to confirm the ordinary category, because Disney prints different numbers on different ships for rooms that share a name.

On the Wish, Treasure and Destiny section of that page, Concierge Royal Suite with Verandah category 1C (stateroom 10166) sleeps 6. Concierge One-Bedroom Suite with Verandah category 2B (stateroom 13510) sleeps 5. Concierge Family Oceanview Stateroom category 3B (stateroom 11504) sleeps 5. Deluxe Family Oceanview with Verandah categories 4B and 4C sleep 4 or 5, and the page notes that the 4-guest versions have two support columns. Deluxe Family Oceanview category 8C (staterooms 7510 and 7512) sleeps 4 or 5, with a trundle only in the rooms that sleep 5. The split that matters: Concierge Family Oceanview with Verandah category 3A (stateroom 13028) sleeps 5 on Disney Wish and Disney Treasure, and 4 on Disney Destiny. The sofa is a double on Wish and Treasure and a single on Destiny. Inside categories in that same section, where a number is printed, sleep 4.

On Disney Dream and Disney Fantasy, the accessible Concierge One-Bedroom Suite category 2B (stateroom 11006) sleeps 5, and the accessible Concierge Family Oceanview with Verandah category 3A (stateroom 11000) sleeps 5. Deluxe Family Oceanview with Verandah categories 4B, 4C and 4E sleep 4 or 5. On Disney Magic and Disney Wonder, the accessible Deluxe Family Oceanview with Verandah category 4E (stateroom 8094) sleeps 5, at 304 sq ft including the verandah, on deck 8. The accessible Concierge One-Bedroom Suite on those two ships sleeps 4, so the suite name that works for five on Dream class does not work for five here. Disney's own Magic and Wonder deck PDF labels Deluxe Family Oceanview with Verandah category 4 as sleeping 5 at 304 sq ft including the verandah, which matches the accessible 4E line. The Disney hub is where the three classes stop sharing deck numbers.

Celebrity

Celebrity is the line where a family of five should not assume a single room exists. Celebrity Edge's stateroom page, checked 21 Nov 2026, prints square footage for AquaClass, Concierge, the Infinite Veranda, singles, verandas, ocean views and insides, and it does not print a guest count on any of them. The one occupancy sentence on that page is about a combination: the Iconic Suite and the Royal Suite connected "comfortably sleeps 10". It also says there are 178 possible connecting options, including two adjoining Edge staterooms with Infinite Verandas joined into one. Connecting is how the page solves a large party. It is not a single cabin that sleeps 5.

The Celebrity Beyond fact sheet dated May 2026 (figures based on the 2027-2028 season) lists 1,646 staterooms and puts 576 of them on a line labelled "Accommodates 3 or 4". It does not add a line for occupancy of 5 or more. It does list two Iconic Suites at 1,892 sq ft plus a 689 sq ft veranda, and it does not say how many people those suites sleep. Celebrity Beyond and Celebrity Ascent are the larger Edge-class hulls. Celebrity Edge is the ship whose own page carries the "sleeps 10" connecting line. None of the three, on the pages opened 21 Nov 2026, names a single stateroom that sleeps 5. A Celebrity PDF whose file name says Ascent, dated March 2026, prints a maiden voyage of 5 April 2020, builder STX France and a double occupancy of 2,886. Those are not the Wikipedia infobox figures for Ascent (in service 22 November 2023, Chantiers de l'Atlantique, 3,260 at double occupancy), so that PDF is not used for a headcount either.

What the number does not decide

A room that sleeps five can still be a bad room. It can sit under a pool deck, beside a connecting door you did not ask for, or in a category the line marks obstructed. Those are the questions on cabins to avoid and how to choose a cabin. Start with the occupancy the line prints, then open the ship.

Frequently asked questions

Can five people share one cruise cabin?

Only if that cabin's own page prints an occupancy of 5 or more. On 21 Nov 2026, Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Norwegian, MSC and Disney each published at least one such room. Celebrity's Edge stateroom page and Beyond fact sheet did not label a single stateroom as sleeping 5. A connecting pair is two cabins.

Does the word family in a cabin name mean it sleeps five?

No. On Norwegian Joy, checked 21 Nov 2026, the Family Suite sleeps up to 6, while the Family Balcony, Family Club Balcony Suite and Family Inside stop at 4. Carnival's Family Harbor blurb names the ships and does not print an occupancy. Read the number, not the adjective.

Which Disney ship treats a party of five differently?

On Disney's accessible-stateroom page, checked 21 Nov 2026, Concierge Family Oceanview with Verandah category 3A sleeps 5 on Disney Wish and Disney Treasure, and 4 on Disney Destiny. The accessible Concierge one-bedroom suite sleeps 5 on Dream and Fantasy, and 4 on Magic and Wonder.

Are Royal Caribbean rooms for five bookable on the website?

Royal Caribbean's FAQ, checked 21 Nov 2026, says staterooms with an occupancy of 5 or more are not available to book on the web. The same FAQ says the line does offer those rooms. This site does not handle the booking.
