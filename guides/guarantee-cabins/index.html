What is a cruise guarantee cabin?

By the Which Cabin editorial team. How we check a page.

Short answer: you book a category, such as inside, ocean view, balcony or suite. The line picks the cabin number and the deck later. You keep the category you booked. You give up choosing a quiet deck, a midship spot, or a clear view. Assignment timing is not the same on every line. The dates below are 20 Nov 2026.

What you get, and what you give up

You get a stateroom in the category the line agreed, or, where the line's own text says so, a higher one. You do not get to refuse the deck afterwards. Choosing a cabin starts with the deck because a balcony under the pool is still under the pool. A guarantee fare skips that step. It is a poor match for a light sleeper or for anyone who needs a particular bed or to be next to another cabin. It is a reasonable match only if the deck truly does not matter to you. That is a description of the trade, not a nudge to book one.

Royal Caribbean, in their words

The guarantee FAQ calls it a Guarantee or "We Pick" booking. The promotional categories on that page are WS for suite or deluxe, XB for balcony, XN for a neighbourhood-view balcony on Oasis and Icon class only, YO for ocean view and ZI for interior. Separate parties may not be berthed together. On Icon of the Seas and the Oasis-class ships, a neighbourhood view is a real outcome, not a rumour. Our Icon page already flags inward-facing balconies for that reason.

Carnival and Norwegian

Carnival's guarantee page is short. You choose the type. They choose the stateroom location and the deck, and they contact you before sailing. Norwegian's Sail Away terms limit the offer to categories IX, OX, BX and MX, and they say assignment can happen at any time up to the day of sailing.

Celebrity and Princess

Celebrity's English booking conditions for other locations, section 1.8, are the page we used. The UK conditions describe the same core rule, so this is not a single-market footnote. Both say the location is allocated any time until port check-in, changes are not accepted after that, and a higher category may be given at Celebrity's discretion. Upper berths, reached by a ladder, are allowed, and the conditions say those berths are for guests over 6, so they suggest not booking a guarantee if you are travelling with young children or need to be near someone else. Princess's pre-cruise FAQ is the outlier on the clock: the number often arrives very late, sometimes at embarkation, and a queen bed is specifically not assured.

Frequently asked questions

When is a guarantee cabin assigned?

Royal Caribbean says any time until port check-in. Carnival says they will contact you prior to sailing and gives no day count. Norwegian's Sail Away terms say any time up to the day of sailing. Celebrity says any time until port check-in. Princess says often close to travel, occasionally at embarkation, and asks you to look about a week out.

Can the line give you a better category?

Celebrity's conditions say they may move you to a higher category and that you cannot require it. Princess says the grade you booked, or higher. Royal Caribbean's FAQ promises the category type you booked and warns the view may be obstructed. None of these pages promise a particular deck.

What do you give up?

The deck, the cabin number and, on several of these pages, an unobstructed view or a particular bed. You also may not be placed next to another party. If those matter, the line's own text says this is the wrong fare.
