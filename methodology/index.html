Sources and methods

Deck layouts

For each ship we use the deck-by-deck listing on CruiseMapper, which summarises what is on every deck of the line's current deck plan, and we link the cruise line's own deck plan page on every ship page. The first 25 ships were checked on 15 Nov 2026, most of the rest on 16 Nov 2026, and a further batch on 18 Nov 2026. Each ship page shows its own checked date.

The deck noise map

We mark each deck as a cabin deck, a public deck or a mix. A cabin deck is marked quiet (green) when the decks directly above and below are also cabin decks with no listed venues. It is marked amber when it shares space with venues, sits directly below pools, sports, buffet or similar areas, or sits directly above bars, shops, theatres, dining or entertainment. Bridge, medical and crew areas are treated as neutral. This is a deck-level shortcut: a venue may only fill part of a deck, so always look up your exact cabin number on the line's deck plan.

Ship facts

Class, builder, entry into service, gross tonnage and guest capacity come from each ship's Wikipedia infobox (which cites registers and line fact sheets) or the line's own pages, as credited on each page. If a figure was missing or unclear, we left it out.

General advice

Motion advice follows the CDC Yellow Book chapter on motion sickness. Other tips (noise from pool decks, elevators, connecting doors, partial-view categories) are our general guidance, not cruise line rules.

What we do not do

We do not copy deck plan images or cruise line text. Diagrams on this site are our own. We do not publish prices or availability, which change daily.

Updates

Ships get refitted and categories change. Report an error and we will re-check the source.
