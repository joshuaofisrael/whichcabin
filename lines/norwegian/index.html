Frequently asked questions

Which Norwegian cabin should I book?

Start with a deck that has cabins directly above and below, midship and away from elevators. The cards below list those quiet decks for each Norwegian ship we cover.

Which Norwegian ships are on this page?

11 ships: Norwegian Prima, Norwegian Viva, Norwegian Encore, Norwegian Getaway, Norwegian Escape, Norwegian Bliss, Norwegian Joy, Norwegian Breakaway, Norwegian Epic, Norwegian Aqua and Norwegian Jewel.

Do these Norwegian ships share cabin decks?

Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy on sister ships. Open a ship for its deck noise map.
