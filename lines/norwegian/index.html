Norwegian Cruise Line: which cabin to book

Cabin guides for 4 Norwegian ships (Prima class, Breakaway Plus class, Breakaway class). Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy across sister ships.

  • Norwegian Prima

    Prima class. Quiet cabin decks: 10 to 13. Under the pool deck: 15 to 18.

  • Norwegian Viva

    Prima class. Quiet cabin decks: 10 to 13. Under the pool deck: 15 to 18.

  • Norwegian Encore

    Breakaway Plus class. Quiet cabin decks: 10 to 13. Under the pool deck: 15, 17 to 19.

  • Norwegian Getaway

    Breakaway class. Quiet cabin decks: 10. Under the pool deck: 14 to 17.
