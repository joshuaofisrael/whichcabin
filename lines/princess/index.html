Princess Cruises: which cabin to book

Cabin guides for 3 Princess ships (Sphere class, Royal class). Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy across sister ships.

  • Sun Princess

    Sphere class. Quiet cabin decks: 11 to 12, 14. Under the pool deck: 16 to 17.

  • Star Princess

    Sphere class. Quiet cabin decks: 11 to 12, 14. Under the pool deck: 16 to 17.

  • Discovery Princess

    Royal class. Quiet cabin decks: 9 to 12, 14. Under the pool deck: 15 to 17.
