Princess Cruises: which cabin to book

By the Which Cabin editorial team. How we check a ship.

Cabin guides for 8 Princess ships (Sphere class, Royal class, Crown class). Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy across sister ships.

Sphere, Royal and Crown do not share a deck 8

Princess on this site is three layouts, and the Royal-class ships are not even one layout. The figures below are the ones already on each ship page (Wikipedia infoboxes, those pages checked 16 Nov 2026). This hub was written 21 Nov 2026 to stop a Sphere answer being pasted onto Ruby. No Princess occupancy page was opened that day, so this hub does not name a cabin that sleeps five. The lines that were checked are in the family-of-five guide.

Sphere class is Sun Princess and Star Princess. Both pages list 177,882 gross tons and 4,300 guests. Sun entered service in February 2024. Star's page lists October 2025. The Sphere notes put Reserve Collection Cabana Mini-Suites on deck 9, the promenade deck, which also has the lobby and Lido Marketplace. Decks 11, 12 and 14 are the sandwiched cabin decks. Deck 10 is one floor above the promenade. Deck 15 sits under deck 16. Deck 17 mixes cabins with the Dome and the lido. Sun's notes add a single-occupancy inside category (IS) on deck 10. Star's notes do not. Do not give Star that solo room unless Princess's own plan shows it.

Royal class splits into three neighbour patterns, which is the detail a class label hides. Discovery Princess (145,000 gross tons, 3,660 guests, in service March 2022) mixes cabins with the lido on both deck 16 and deck 17. Enchanted Princess and Sky Princess (both 145,281 gross tons and 3,660 guests) put the lido cabins on deck 16 and the kids' and teen areas on deck 17. Royal Princess (142,714 gross tons, 3,600 guests) and Majestic Princess (144,216 gross tons, 3,560 guests) put cabins with the lido on deck 16 and give deck 17 to the sundeck, the adults' area, kids and teens, with no cabins on 17. Deck 8 on all of these is the first cabin deck above the Promenade on deck 7. "Deck 17" is a cabin deck on Discovery, Enchanted and Sky, and it is not a cabin deck on Royal or Majestic.

Ruby Princess is the Crown-class ship, listed at 113,561 gross tons and 3,080 guests, in service November 2008. Cabins start on deck 5, mixed with the plaza, dining and the lobby, under the casino and shops on deck 6. Deck 14 mixes cabins with the bridge and a pool. Deck 15 mixes cabins with the lido, under the sundeck and spa on deck 16. A Sun Princess deck number does not transfer. The quietest-decks table is the ship-by-ship list.

Frequently asked questions

Do all Royal-class Princess ships put cabins on deck 17?

No. Discovery, Enchanted and Sky mix cabins onto deck 17. Royal and Majestic give deck 17 to the sundeck, the adults' area, kids and teens, with no cabins. Deck 16 is the lido cabin deck on all five.

Is Sun Princess the same layout as Ruby Princess?

No. Sun and Star are Sphere class, at 177,882 gross tons, with cabana mini-suites on the promenade deck. Ruby is Crown class, at 113,561 gross tons, with cabins starting on deck 5 beside the plaza. The deck numbers are not advice you can carry from one to the other.

Which Princess cabin should I book?

Start with a deck that has cabins directly above and below, midship and away from elevators. The cards below list those quiet decks for each Princess ship we cover.

Which Princess ships are on this page?

8 ships: Sun Princess, Star Princess, Discovery Princess, Enchanted Princess, Sky Princess, Royal Princess, Majestic Princess and Ruby Princess.

Do these Princess ships share cabin decks?
