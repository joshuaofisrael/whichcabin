Virgin Voyages: which cabin to book

By the Which Cabin editorial team. How we check a ship.

Cabin guides for 4 Virgin ships. Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy across sister ships.

Virgin does not sell a normal inside cabin

Virgin Voyages' own cabins page, checked 20 Nov 2026, names four places to sleep, with sizes, and it does not list a standard inside room. Insider cabins are 105 to 177 square feet for 1 to 4 people, and the description does not mention a sea view. Sea View cabins are 130 to 190 square feet for 1 to 3 people, with a porthole or window seat. Sea Terrace cabins are 185 to 225 square feet for 2 to 4 people, and the page says almost all of them have a hammock on the terrace. RockStar Quarters, the suites, run from 352 to 2,147 square feet for 2 to 4 people. Those ranges are the line's, not ours. A Sea Terrace is the room most passengers are choosing between, and it is already a balcony-style cabin, so the deck matters more than the usual inside-versus-balcony argument.

One layout across four ships

Scarlet Lady, Valiant Lady, Resilient Lady and Brilliant Lady are the four Virgin ships on this site. The ship pages list the same builder, Fincantieri, the same 110,000 gross tons and the same 2,770 guests, with service dates of February 2020, March 2022, May 2023 and September 2025. Virgin's Scarlet Lady page on the line's site, checked 20 Nov 2026, calls that ship the first and mentions a revamp. It does not date the work, so this hub does not pretend the four deck plans have diverged.

The deck-by-deck labels behind our ship pages, checked 16 Nov 2026, put cabins with the spa and a dining room on deck 5. Decks 6 and 7 are the lounge, casino, shops and lobby, with no cabins. Decks 8 to 14 are cabin decks, and the bridge is on deck 14. Deck 15 mixes suites with the lido pools. Decks 16 and 17 are sports, sun and a jogging track. For a light sleeper who is not in a suite, the middle of that cabin run (decks 9 to 13) has cabins above and below. Deck 8 is the first cabin deck over the public rooms. Deck 14 sits under the pool deck. Open Scarlet Lady for the map, then Brilliant Lady if you want the newest hull with the same pattern. The quietest-decks table lists the sandwiched decks for each.

Frequently asked questions

Does Virgin Voyages have inside cabins?

Virgin's cabins page, checked 20 Nov 2026, lists Insider, Sea View, Sea Terrace and RockStar Quarters. Insider is the category with no sea view in the description, at 105 to 177 square feet. It is not labelled as a windowless inside room of the sort Carnival or Royal Caribbean sell.

Do Scarlet Lady and Brilliant Lady use the same cabin decks?

On the deck listings checked 16 Nov 2026, yes. Both put public rooms on decks 6 and 7, cabin decks on 8 to 14, and suites with the pools on deck 15. Service dates differ. The deck pattern on our pages does not.

Which Virgin cabin should I book?

Start with a deck that has cabins directly above and below, midship and away from elevators. The cards below list those quiet decks for each Virgin ship we cover.

Which Virgin ships are on this page?

4 ships: Scarlet Lady, Valiant Lady, Resilient Lady and Brilliant Lady.
