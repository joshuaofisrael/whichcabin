Holland America Line: which cabin to book

By the Which Cabin editorial team. How we check a ship.

Cabin guides for 5 Holland America ships (Pinnacle class, Signature class). Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy across sister ships.

Pinnacle class and Signature class are not the same quiet deck

Holland America on this site is five ships and two classes. Rotterdam, Nieuw Statendam and Koningsdam are Pinnacle class. Eurodam and Nieuw Amsterdam are Signature class. The numbers below are the ones already on each ship page, from the Wikipedia infoboxes those pages were checked against on 16 Nov 2026. This hub was written 21 Nov 2026 to put the two classes side by side. It is not a new measurement of the ships, and it does not claim a cabin that sleeps five, because no Holland America occupancy page was opened that day. That question, for the lines that were checked, is the family-of-five guide.

Pinnacle class, on the deck labels: deck 1 mixes cabins with the lobby. Decks 2 and 3 are lounges, shops, the casino and dining, with no cabins. Decks 4 to 8 are cabin decks, and the bridge is on deck 8. Deck 9 is the lido, pools, spa and sundeck, so deck 8 is the cabin deck directly under it. Deck 10 mixes cabins with the kids' and teen areas. Deck 11 mixes cabins with sports. Deck 12 is the Explorations Lounge, The Retreat and an adults' sundeck, with no cabins. The guest counts already on the pages are 2,668 on Rotterdam (99,935 gross tons), 2,666 on Nieuw Statendam (99,902) and 2,650 at double occupancy on Koningsdam (99,863). Close, and not identical.

Signature class keeps the low public block and changes the top. Deck 1 still mixes cabins with the lobby, and decks 2 and 3 are still the promenade, casino, lounge, shops and dining. Decks 4 to 8 are cabin decks, and deck 7 also lists a suite lounge, which the Pinnacle notes do not. Deck 9 is the lido, so deck 8 is again directly under it. Deck 10 mixes cabins with the kids' and teen areas. Deck 11 mixes cabins with the observation area, sports and the Crow's Nest, instead of stopping at a Retreat deck with no cabins. Eurodam is listed at 86,273 gross tons and 2,104 guests. Nieuw Amsterdam is listed at 86,700 gross tons and 2,106 guests.

Eurodam has a date the others do not. Its ship page already notes that Holland America posts a separate deck plan for sailings after 24 March 2027, and that this site follows the current listing. That sentence is Eurodam's. It is not copied onto Nieuw Amsterdam. The cabins to avoid guide is the general version of the "deck under the lido" point, which on both of these classes is deck 8.

Frequently asked questions

Is deck 8 a quiet cabin on Holland America?

Not on these five ships. On both Pinnacle class and Signature class, deck 9 is the lido, so deck 8 sits directly under the pools. The sandwiched cabin decks are lower in the run, from deck 4 upward, and deck 7 on Signature class also lists a suite lounge.

Does Eurodam keep this deck plan after March 2027?

Holland America's Eurodam page links a separate deck plan for sailings after 24 March 2027. The map on our Eurodam page follows the current listing. Nieuw Amsterdam's page does not carry that date.

Which Holland America cabin should I book?

Start with a deck that has cabins directly above and below, midship and away from elevators. The cards below list those quiet decks for each Holland America ship we cover.

Which Holland America ships are on this page?

5 ships: Rotterdam, Nieuw Statendam, Koningsdam, Eurodam and Nieuw Amsterdam.

Do these Holland America ships share cabin decks?
