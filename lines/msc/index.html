MSC Cruises: which cabin to book

Cabin guides for 9 MSC ships (World class, Seaside EVO class, Meraviglia class, Meraviglia Plus class, Seaside class, Fantasia class). Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy across sister ships.

  • MSC World America

    World class. Quiet cabin decks: 10 to 12, 14 to 15. Under the pool deck: 16, 19, 21.

  • MSC Seascape

    Seaside EVO class. Quiet cabin decks: 10 to 14. Under the pool deck: 15 to 16, 18 to 19.

Frequently asked questions

Which MSC cabin should I book?

Start with a deck that has cabins directly above and below, midship and away from elevators. The cards below list those quiet decks for each MSC ship we cover.

Which MSC ships are on this page?

9 ships: MSC World America, MSC Seascape, MSC Seashore, MSC World Europa, MSC Meraviglia, MSC Grandiosa, MSC Virtuosa, MSC Seaside and MSC Divina.

Do these MSC ships share cabin decks?

Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy on sister ships. Open a ship for its deck noise map.
