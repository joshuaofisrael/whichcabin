Disney Cruise Line: which cabin to book

By the Which Cabin editorial team. How we check a ship.

Cabin guides for 7 Disney ships (Wish class, Dream class, Magic class). Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy across sister ships.

Three Disney classes, three different quiet decks

The seven Disney ships here are not one layout with the name swapped. Wish, Treasure and Destiny are Wish class. Dream and Fantasy are Dream class. Magic and Wonder are the smaller Magic class. The deck notes on each ship page come from that ship's deck-by-deck listing. This hub only lines the three patterns up so a Wish-class answer is not copied onto Magic.

On the Wish-class notes, decks 7, 8 and 9 are the quiet core, with cabin decks above and below. Deck 2 shares the deck with the kids' clubs and sits under dining on deck 3. Concierge rooms are on decks 10 to 13, near the pools, which is convenient and busier. Start with Disney Wish if that is the ship, and do not assume Disney Treasure has invented a new deck order. The Wish-class notes are shared because the listings match, and each page still has its own facts table.

Dream class is a different stack. Deck 2 mixes cabins with Enchanted Garden and sits under the lobby, shops and dining on deck 3. Deck 5 mixes cabins with the kids' and teen clubs. Decks 6 to 10 are cabin decks, with the bridge on deck 10, under the lido, pools and spa on deck 11. Disney Dream is the page to open. Fantasy uses the same Dream-class notes.

Magic class is smaller again. Wikipedia figures already on the ship pages give Magic 83,338 gross tons and 1,750 guests at double occupancy, and Wonder 84,130 gross tons. Deck 5 mixes cabins with the kids' club and sits above the promenade and shops on deck 4. Deck 9 is the lido, pools and spa, so deck 8 is the cabin deck directly underneath, and deck 10 is the adults' area, sports and a waterslide. Read Disney Magic before you treat a Wish-class "deck 8" tip as if it meant the same thing here. The cabins to avoid guide is the general version of that overhead-noise point.

Frequently asked questions

Is deck 8 a quiet cabin deck on every Disney ship?

No. On the Wish-class ships in this guide, decks 7 to 9 are the sandwiched cabin decks. On Magic and Wonder, deck 8 sits directly under the lido on deck 9. The deck number is not the advice. The neighbour above it is.

Which Disney ships share a layout?

Wish, Treasure and Destiny share the Wish-class notes. Dream and Fantasy share the Dream-class notes. Magic and Wonder share the Magic-class notes. Open the ship, not the line, before you pick a deck.

Which Disney cabin should I book?

Start with a deck that has cabins directly above and below, midship and away from elevators. The cards below list those quiet decks for each Disney ship we cover.

Which Disney ships are on this page?

7 ships: Disney Wish, Disney Treasure, Disney Destiny, Disney Dream, Disney Fantasy, Disney Magic and Disney Wonder.

Do these Disney ships share cabin decks?

Ships of the same class share a layout, so the same decks tend to be quiet or noisy on sister ships. Open a ship for its deck noise map.
