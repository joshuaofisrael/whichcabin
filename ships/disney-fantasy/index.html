Which cabin to book on Disney Fantasy

Disney Cruise Line · Dream class · deck plans checked 16 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: decks 7 to 9. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 2, 5), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: deck 10 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and deck 5 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Disney Fantasy has 7 decks with passenger cabins. Below is our deck noise map, which marks every deck by what is on it and what is directly above and below, followed by ship-specific tips and the cabins to think twice about. It is a planning shortcut: the line's own deck plan is always the final word on any cabin number.

Best cabins on Disney Fantasy by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on decks 7 to 9, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 2, 5, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: decks near the kids' areas (deck 5) save walking, but they are busier.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

Disney Fantasy tips

Deck 2 shares a restaurant

Deck 2 mixes cabins with Enchanted Garden and sits directly below the lobby, shops and dining on deck 3.

Kids and teens on deck 5

Deck 5 mixes cabins with the kids' and teen clubs. Decks 6 to 10 are cabin decks, with the bridge on deck 10, under the lido, pools and spa on deck 11.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 10 sits directly below Lido, Pools. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 5 sits directly above Lounge, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 2 shares the deck with Enchanted Garden. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best deck to stay on Disney Fantasy?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on decks 7 to 9, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 8 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Disney Fantasy?

Avoid deck 10 if you dislike noise overhead, because it sits directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about deck 5, which sits above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Disney Fantasy for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 2, 5). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Disney Fantasy?

7 decks have passenger cabins: 2, 5 to 10, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Disney Fantasy?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
