Which cabin to book on Icon of the Seas

Royal Caribbean · Icon class · deck plans checked 15 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: deck 10. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 3 to 4), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: decks 14, 16 to 18 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and decks 4, 7 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Icon of the Seas has 12 decks with passenger cabins. Below is our deck noise map, which marks every deck by what is on it and what is directly above and below, followed by ship-specific tips and the cabins to think twice about. It is a planning shortcut: the line's own deck plan is always the final word on any cabin number.

Best cabins on Icon of the Seas by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on deck 10, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 3 to 4, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: decks near the kids' areas (decks 6 to 7) save walking, but they are busier.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

Icon of the Seas tips

Surfside is built for small children

Surfside, at the stern on deck 7, is the neighbourhood designed for families with children under six, with a kids' water area, a carousel and family cabins nearby. Ideal if you have toddlers; skip it if you don't.

Check the balcony type

Icon-class plans include Infinite Balcony categories (I1, I3, IF, IB) on decks 9 to 12 alongside classic balconies. They are not the same layout as a standard balcony, so read the line's room description before choosing.

Inward-facing balconies

Categories marked Inward Facing Balcony (for example H3 to H5 on decks 12 and 14) look into the ship's neighbourhoods rather than out to sea.

Low decks near the big venues

Cabins on decks 3 and 4 share space with the Music Hall, the main dining room, the casino and the Absolute Zero ice arena. Fine for heavy sleepers on a budget; not the pick for light sleepers.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 14 sits directly below AquaDome, Chill Island, Pools. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 16 sits directly below Chill Island, Pool, Sports. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 17 sits directly below Sundeck, Pool. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 18 sits directly below Suite Sundeck. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 4 sits directly above Dining, Music Hall, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 7 sits directly above Royal Promenade, Kids, Teens, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 3 shares the deck with Dining, Music Hall. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 8 shares the deck with Central Park. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 12 shares the deck with Spa. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best deck to stay on Icon of the Seas?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on deck 10, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 10 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Icon of the Seas?

Avoid decks 14, 16 to 18 if you dislike noise overhead, because they sit directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about decks 4, 7, which sit above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Icon of the Seas for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 3 to 4). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Icon of the Seas?

12 decks have passenger cabins: 3 to 4, 7 to 12, 14, 16 to 18, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Icon of the Seas?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
