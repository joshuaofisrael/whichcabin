Which cabin to book on Disney Wish

Disney Cruise Line · Wish class · deck plans checked 15 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: decks 7 to 9. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 2, 6), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: decks 10 to 12, 14 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and deck 6 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Disney Wish has 11 decks with passenger cabins. Below is our deck noise map, which marks every deck by what is on it and what is directly above and below, followed by ship-specific tips and the cabins to think twice about. It is a planning shortcut: the line's own deck plan is always the final word on any cabin number.

Best cabins on Disney Wish by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on decks 7 to 9, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 2, 6, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: decks near the kids' areas (decks 2, 12) save walking, but they are busier.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

Disney Wish tips

Deck 2 is near the kids' clubs

Deck 2 cabins share the deck with the kids' clubs and sit directly below dining venues on deck 3.

Decks 7 to 9 are the quiet core

Decks 7, 8 and 9 have cabin decks above and below, the best choice for light sleepers and for families with nap-time toddlers.

Concierge rooms are by the pools

Concierge categories (1 to 3) are on decks 10 to 13, near the pool decks, with the two-storey Tower Suite on decks 14 and 15.

Family rooms

Deluxe Family Oceanview and Verandah categories (4A to 4E, 8B and 8C) sleep more people and are spread across decks 6 to 11.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 10 sits directly below Pools, Sundeck. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 11 sits directly below Lido, Pools, Sundeck. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 12 sits directly below Pools, Sundeck, Waterslide. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 14 sits directly below Waterslides. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 6 sits directly above Spa, Sports, Teens, Dining, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 2 shares the deck with Kids. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 13 shares the deck with Adults, Pools, Sundeck, Waterslide. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 15 shares the deck with Waterslides. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best deck to stay on Disney Wish?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on decks 7 to 9, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 8 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Disney Wish?

Avoid decks 10 to 12, 14 if you dislike noise overhead, because they sit directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about deck 6, which sits above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Disney Wish for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 2, 6). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Disney Wish?

11 decks have passenger cabins: 2, 6 to 15, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Disney Wish?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
