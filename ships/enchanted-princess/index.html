Which cabin to book on Enchanted Princess

Princess Cruises · Royal class · deck plans checked 16 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: decks 9 to 12, 14. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 8 to 9), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: decks 15 to 17 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and deck 8 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Enchanted Princess has 9 decks with passenger cabins. Below is our deck noise map, which marks every deck by what is on it and what is directly above and below, followed by ship-specific tips and the cabins to think twice about. It is a planning shortcut: the line's own deck plan is always the final word on any cabin number.

Best cabins on Enchanted Princess by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on decks 9 to 12, 14, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 8 to 9, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: decks near the kids' areas (deck 17) save walking, but they are busier.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

Enchanted Princess tips

Deck 8 sits above the Promenade

Deck 8 (Emerald) is the first cabin deck above the Promenade deck (7), with its bars and shops.

Upper cabin decks meet the pools

Deck 16 mixes cabins with the lido pools, and deck 17 mixes cabins with the sundeck and the kids' and teen areas.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 15 sits directly below Lido, Pools. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 16 sits directly below Sundeck. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 17 sits directly below Sports. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 8 sits directly above Promenade, Shops, which can stay busy late.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best deck to stay on Enchanted Princess?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on decks 9 to 12, 14, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 11 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Enchanted Princess?

Avoid decks 15 to 17 if you dislike noise overhead, because they sit directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about deck 8, which sits above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Enchanted Princess for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 8 to 9). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Enchanted Princess?

9 decks have passenger cabins: 8 to 12, 14 to 17, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Enchanted Princess?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
