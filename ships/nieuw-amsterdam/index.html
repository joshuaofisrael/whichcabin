Which cabin to book on Nieuw Amsterdam

Holland America Line · Signature class · deck plans checked 16 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: deck 5. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 1, 4), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: decks 8, 10 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and deck 4 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Nieuw Amsterdam has 8 decks with passenger cabins. Below is our deck noise map, which marks every deck by what is on it and what is directly above and below, followed by ship-specific tips and the cabins to think twice about. It is a planning shortcut: the line's own deck plan is always the final word on any cabin number.

Best cabins on Nieuw Amsterdam by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on deck 5, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 1, 4, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: decks near the kids' areas (deck 10) save walking, but they are busier.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

Nieuw Amsterdam tips

Deck 1 includes the lobby

Deck 1 mixes cabins with the lobby. Decks 2 and 3 are the promenade, casino, lounge, shops and dining.

Deck 8 is under the lido

Decks 4 to 8 are cabin decks, and deck 7 also lists a suite lounge. Deck 9 is the lido, pools and spa, so deck 8 is directly under it.

Kids on deck 10, Crow's Nest on deck 11

Deck 10 mixes cabins with the kids' and teen areas. Deck 11 mixes cabins with the observation area, sports and the Crow's Nest.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 8 sits directly below Lido, Pools. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 10 sits directly below Sports. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 4 sits directly above Promenade, Shops, Dining, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 1 shares the deck with Lobby. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 7 shares the deck with Suite Lounge. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 11 shares the deck with Observation, Sports, Crows Nest. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best deck to stay on Nieuw Amsterdam?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on deck 5, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 5 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Nieuw Amsterdam?

Avoid decks 8, 10 if you dislike noise overhead, because they sit directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about deck 4, which sits above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Nieuw Amsterdam for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 1, 4). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Nieuw Amsterdam?

8 decks have passenger cabins: 1, 4 to 8, 10 to 11, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Nieuw Amsterdam?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
