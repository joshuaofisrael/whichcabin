Which cabin to book on Celebrity Ascent

Celebrity Cruises · Edge class · deck plans checked 15 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: decks 8 to 11. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 3, 6), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: deck 12 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and decks 6 to 7 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Celebrity Ascent has 9 decks with passenger cabins. Below is our deck noise map, which marks every deck by what is on it and what is directly above and below, followed by ship-specific tips and the cabins to think twice about. It is a planning shortcut: the line's own deck plan is always the final word on any cabin number.

Best cabins on Celebrity Ascent by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on decks 8 to 11, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 3, 6, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: a midship cabin on a quiet deck, close to a bank of elevators.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

Celebrity Ascent tips

Know the Infinite Veranda

Edge-class 'Edge Stateroom with Infinite Veranda' cabins (E1, E2 and single ES) build the veranda space into the room behind a window wall that opens, instead of a separate outdoor balcony. If you want a classic outdoor balcony with a chair and table, look at Sunset Veranda, Concierge Class or the suites instead.

Avoid the partial-view codes

Celebrity lists partial-view versions of some categories, such as E3 and C3. If the view matters, book the non-partial category.

Deck 12 is under the pools

Deck 12 is the top cabin deck and sits directly below deck 14 (there is no deck 13), home to the main pool, spa and sundeck.

The Magic Carpet

The Magic Carpet is a platform that moves up and down the starboard side, used as a bar, restaurant and tender platform. It is part of the ship's profile, so check the deck plan if you are booking a starboard cabin near midship.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 12 sits directly below Lido, Pools, Sundeck. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 6 sits directly above Shops, Eden, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 7 sits directly above Eden, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 3 shares the deck with Restaurants, Lobby. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 15 shares the deck with Rooftop Garden. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best deck to stay on Celebrity Ascent?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on decks 8 to 11, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 10 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Celebrity Ascent?

Avoid deck 12 if you dislike noise overhead, because it sits directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about decks 6 to 7, which sit above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Celebrity Ascent for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 3, 6). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Celebrity Ascent?

9 decks have passenger cabins: 3, 6 to 12, 15, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Celebrity Ascent?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
