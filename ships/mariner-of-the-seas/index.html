Which cabin to book on Mariner of the Seas

Royal Caribbean · Voyager class · deck plans checked 20 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: decks 7 to 9. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 2 to 3), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: decks 10, 12 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and decks 3, 6 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Best cabins on Mariner of the Seas by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on decks 7 to 9, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 2 to 3, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: decks near the kids' areas (deck 12) save walking, but they are busier.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

What is different on Mariner of the Seas

Shorter, earlier, and a different class

Mariner of the Seas entered service on 16 November 2003, built at Kvaerner Masa-Yards in Turku. Wikipedia's infobox, checked 20 Nov 2026, lists 139,863 gross tons, a length of 311.12 metres and a maximum beam of 48.0 metres, and it shows capacity as 3,114 passengers without calling that figure double occupancy. Freedom of the Seas, checked the same day, is a later Freedom-class ship: 338.774 metres, 156,271 gross tons since 2015, and 3,782 guests at double occupancy. Treat the shared 'ice rink below, pools above' pattern as a family resemblance, then use the numbers and the deck labels below.

The 2018 drydock moved cabins, not just slides

CruiseMapper's refurbishment review dates Mariner's Royal Amplified drydock to 8 May through 24 June 2018 in Freeport. On deck 3, The Golden Room and Dragon's Lair, both nightclubs, were converted into staterooms. Deck 4, which has no cabins in the current label, became Izumi and Playmakers. Deck 12 aft gained the Slashaway Bay children's aqua park in place of the outdoor youth area. Deck 15 lost the Skylight Chapel and gained The Observatorium escape room. Deck 13 gained the Cyclone and Typhoon slides and a FlowRider. The new deck 3 cabins are the detail that changes a booking: they sit on a deck that still lists Studio B and a dining room, not in the quiet run of decks 6 to 10.

A March 2026 refit, with the same cabin decks

CruiseMapper's Mariner overview, checked 20 Nov 2026, dates a later refurbishment to March 2026. The deck labels that day still match this page: deck 2 cabins with the ice rink, deck 4 casino, lounge and dining with no cabins, deck 5 promenade plus a dining room Freedom's label does not list, and deck 15 an escape room with no cabins. Until Royal Caribbean's own plan shows a cabin deck added or removed, the noise map stands, with the March 2026 date noted beside it.

Mariner of the Seas tips

Low decks sit with the ice rink and Studio B

Deck 2 mixes cabins with the ice rink, and deck 3 mixes cabins with Studio B. Deck 4 above that is the casino and lounge, with no cabins.

The pool deck is a cabin deck

Deck 11 mixes cabins with the lido pools. Deck 12 mixes cabins with the kids' and teen areas and the spa, under the sports deck.

Deck 15's chapel became an escape room

Mariner's 2018 Royal Amplified refit replaced the Skylight Chapel on deck 15 with The Observatorium, a timed escape-room attraction (Puzzle Break), according to CruiseMapper's deck plan notes. Deck 15 has no cabins either way.

Dining shares the low decks

CruiseMapper's Mariner deck listing puts dining rooms on decks 3, 4 and 5 with Studio B, the casino/lounge and the promenade. Adventure of the Seas keeps dining off those mid-public decks in its listing, so Mariner's cabin neighbours on decks 2 and 3 sit closer to mealtime traffic.

Entered service later than Adventure

Mariner entered service in November 2003 (Wikipedia infobox, checked 19 Nov 2026), two years after Adventure of the Seas. Gross tonnage is listed as 139,863, a touch larger than Adventure's 138,193.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 10 sits directly below Lido, Pools, Sundeck. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 12 sits directly below Sports, FlowRider, Waterslides, Golf. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 3 sits directly above Ice Rink, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 6 sits directly above Promenade, Lobby, Shops, Dining, Helideck, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 2 shares the deck with Ice Rink. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 11 shares the deck with Lido, Pools, Sundeck. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

Did Mariner of the Seas add cabins in the 2018 refit?

CruiseMapper's 2018 drydock review says The Golden Room and Dragon's Lair on deck 3 were converted into staterooms. Those cabins share deck 3 with Studio B and a dining room. Deck 4 did not gain cabins. It is where Izumi and Playmakers went.

Was Mariner of the Seas refurbished in 2026?

CruiseMapper's ship overview, checked 20 Nov 2026, dates the latest refurbishment to March 2026. The deck labels that day still match the noise map on this page, including the escape room on deck 15 and cabins beside the ice rink on deck 2.

What is the best deck to stay on Mariner of the Seas?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on decks 7 to 9, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 8 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Mariner of the Seas?

Avoid decks 10, 12 if you dislike noise overhead, because they sit directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about decks 3, 6, which sit above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Mariner of the Seas for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 2 to 3). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Mariner of the Seas?

9 decks have passenger cabins: 2 to 3, 6 to 12, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Mariner of the Seas?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
