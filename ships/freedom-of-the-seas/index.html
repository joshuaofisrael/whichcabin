Which cabin to book on Freedom of the Seas

Royal Caribbean · Freedom class · deck plans checked 20 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: decks 7 to 9. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 2 to 3), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: decks 10, 12 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and decks 3, 6 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Best cabins on Freedom of the Seas by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on decks 7 to 9, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 2 to 3, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: decks near the kids' areas (deck 12) save walking, but they are busier.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

What is different on Freedom of the Seas

A larger ship than Mariner, not a copy of it

Freedom of the Seas is Freedom class, not Voyager class. Wikipedia's infobox, checked 20 Nov 2026, lists a length of 338.774 metres, gross tonnage of 156,271 since 2015 (154,407 from 2006 to 2015), and 3,782 guests at double occupancy, with a maximum of 4,515. Mariner of the Seas, on the same check, is listed at 311.12 metres and 139,863 gross tons, with capacity shown as 3,114 passengers and not labelled double occupancy. The waterline beam is about 38.6 metres on both infoboxes. Freedom's extreme beam, including the bridge wings, is listed at 56.08 metres, against Mariner's maximum beam of 48.0 metres. The quiet-deck shortcut can look similar. The ships are not the same size.

Deck labels re-checked 20 Nov 2026

CruiseMapper's Freedom of the Seas deck-plan index, opened 20 Nov 2026, still labels deck 4 as casino, lounge and dining, with no cabins. Liberty of the Seas puts cabins on that deck. Freedom's deck 5 label is promenade, lobby, shops and the helideck, with no dining room in the label. Mariner's deck 5 label on the same day includes a third dining room. Freedom's deck 15 label is still Royal Escape Room, and that deck has no cabins. The cabin-deck map on this page did not need a redraw.

What the 2020 refit changed, and what January 2025 did not

CruiseMapper's Amplified notes, still on that page, record the conversions already easy to miss on a class diagram: the deck 2 conference center became 25 inside cabins, the deck 3 On Air Club became 12 inside cabins, the deck 6 loyalty desk became 4 inside cabins, and an aft suite on deck 6 was replaced with 4 balcony cabins. The new deck 2 rooms share a deck with the ice rink. The new deck 3 rooms share a deck with Studio B and a dining room. CruiseMapper's overview also dates a later refurbishment to January 2025. It does not show a new cabin deck in the 20 Nov 2026 labels, so this page keeps the same noise map and records the date.

Freedom of the Seas tips

Low decks sit with the ice rink and Studio B

Deck 2 mixes cabins with the ice rink, and deck 3 mixes cabins with Studio B.

The pool deck is a cabin deck

Deck 11 mixes cabins with the lido pools and sundeck. Deck 12 mixes cabins with the spa and the kids' and teen areas, and it sits under the sports deck.

2020 Royal Amplified added cabins on decks 2, 3 and 6

CruiseMapper's Freedom of the Seas deck plan notes (refit completed March 2020) record that the Deck 2 conference center became 25 inside cabins, the Deck 3 On Air Club became 12 inside cabins, and the Deck 6 loyalty desk became 4 inside cabins. Those new insides sit on decks that already mix cabins with the ice rink (deck 2) or Studio B and dining (deck 3), so treat them like any other cabin beside late venues: check the exact cabin number on the line's deck plan.

Sports deck thrills sit above deck 12

The same 2020 drydock added the Cyclone and Typhoon waterslides on deck 13 beside the FlowRider. Deck 12 remains the cabin deck under that sports deck, so morning and afternoon activity overhead is the main cabin trade-off after the Amplified refit.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 10 sits directly below Lido, Pools, Sundeck. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 12 sits directly below Sports, FlowRider, Waterslides, Golf. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 3 sits directly above Ice Rink, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 6 sits directly above Promenade, Lobby, Shops, Helideck, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 2 shares the deck with Ice Rink. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 11 shares the deck with Lido, Pools, Sundeck. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

Is Freedom of the Seas the same layout as Mariner of the Seas?

No. Freedom is Freedom class and longer: Wikipedia lists 338.774 metres and 156,271 gross tons, against Mariner's 311.12 metres and 139,863 gross tons. CruiseMapper's labels on 20 Nov 2026 also differ on deck 5 (no dining room in Freedom's label) and on deck 4 (no cabins on Freedom; Liberty, not Mariner, is the sister with cabins there).

Did Freedom of the Seas change cabins after the 2020 refit?

CruiseMapper's Amplified notes say yes: inside cabins replaced the deck 2 conference center, the deck 3 On Air Club and the deck 6 loyalty desk, and an aft suite on deck 6 became balcony cabins. A later refurbishment is dated January 2025 on the same site. The 20 Nov 2026 deck labels do not add a new cabin deck.

What is the best deck to stay on Freedom of the Seas?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on decks 7 to 9, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 8 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Freedom of the Seas?

Avoid decks 10, 12 if you dislike noise overhead, because they sit directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about decks 3, 6, which sit above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Freedom of the Seas for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 2 to 3). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Freedom of the Seas?

9 decks have passenger cabins: 2 to 3, 6 to 12, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Freedom of the Seas?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
