Which cabin to book on Norwegian Getaway

Norwegian Cruise Line · Breakaway class · deck plans checked 15 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: deck 10. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 5, 8), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: decks 14 to 17 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and decks 8 to 9 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Norwegian Getaway has 11 decks with passenger cabins. Below is our deck noise map, which marks every deck by what is on it and what is directly above and below, followed by ship-specific tips and the cabins to think twice about. It is a planning shortcut: the line's own deck plan is always the final word on any cabin number.

Best cabins on Norwegian Getaway by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on deck 10, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 5, 8, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: decks near the kids' areas (decks 12 to 13) save walking, but they are busier.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

Norwegian Getaway tips

Solo travellers: the Studios

Studio cabins for solo travellers are on decks 10 to 12 on Norwegian Encore and 10 to 11 on Norwegian Getaway, according to CruiseMapper's deck plans. Getaway's plan also shows a Studio Lounge reserved for studio guests.

Deck 8 is mixed

Deck 8 has cabins plus the indoor promenade shops, and it sits above the entertainment and casino deck (7). Deck 9 cabins are one floor above those shops and bars.

The Haven is high up

The Haven suites and their private sundeck are on the upper decks, mixed in with the main pool and sports areas.

Kids' areas on decks 12 and 13

On Norwegian Getaway, CruiseMapper's deck plan places kids' areas on cabin decks 12 and 13. Handy for families; others may prefer decks 9 to 11.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 14 sits directly below Lido, Pools. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 15 sits directly below Adults Sundeck. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 16 sits directly below Sports, Waterslides. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 17 sits directly below Sports, Sundeck. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 8 sits directly above Entertainment, Casino, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 9 sits directly above Promenade, Shops, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 12 shares the deck with Kids. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 13 shares the deck with Kids. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best deck to stay on Norwegian Getaway?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on deck 10, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 10 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Norwegian Getaway?

Avoid decks 14 to 17 if you dislike noise overhead, because they sit directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about decks 8 to 9, which sit above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Norwegian Getaway for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 5, 8). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Norwegian Getaway?

11 decks have passenger cabins: 5, 8 to 17, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Norwegian Getaway?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
