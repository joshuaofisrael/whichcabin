Which cabin to book on MSC Seaside

MSC Cruises · Seaside class · deck plans checked 16 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: decks 12 to 13. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 5, 9), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: decks 15, 18 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and deck 16 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

MSC Seaside has 10 decks with passenger cabins. Below is our deck noise map, which marks every deck by what is on it and what is directly above and below, followed by ship-specific tips and the cabins to think twice about. It is a planning shortcut: the line's own deck plan is always the final word on any cabin number.

Best cabins on MSC Seaside by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on decks 12 to 13, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 5, 9, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: decks near the kids' areas (deck 18) save walking, but they are busier.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

MSC Seaside tips

Deck 9 is above the lido

On this ship the promenade is deck 6. Deck 8 is the lido and spa, and deck 9 is the first cabin deck above that.

Yacht Club shares the pool deck

Deck 16 lists suites, the Yacht Club lounge, a sundeck and the lido pools together. The waterslides and adventure trail are on deck 20.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 15 sits directly below Sundeck, Lido, Pools. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 18 sits directly below Yacht Club Sundeck, Sports. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 16 sits directly above Venice Beach, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 5 shares the deck with Lobby. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 10 shares the deck with Maspalomas. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best deck to stay on MSC Seaside?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on decks 12 to 13, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 13 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on MSC Seaside?

Avoid decks 15, 18 if you dislike noise overhead, because they sit directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about deck 16, which sits above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on MSC Seaside for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 5, 9). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on MSC Seaside?

10 decks have passenger cabins: 5, 9 to 16, 18, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on MSC Seaside?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
