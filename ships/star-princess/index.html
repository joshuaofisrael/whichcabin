Which cabin to book on Star Princess

Princess Cruises · Sphere class · deck plans checked 15 Nov 2026

Quick answer

  • Light sleepers: decks 11 to 12, 14. These cabin decks have cabins directly above and below.
  • Seasickness: a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 5 to 6), away from the bow.
  • Think twice: decks 16 to 17 (directly under pool, sports or buffet areas) and decks 9 to 10 (over bars, shops or entertainment).

Star Princess has 10 decks with passenger cabins. Below is our deck noise map, which marks every deck by what is on it and what is directly above and below, followed by ship-specific tips and the cabins to think twice about. It is a planning shortcut: the line's own deck plan is always the final word on any cabin number.

Best cabins on Star Princess by traveller

  • Light sleepers: cabins on decks 11 to 12, 14, ideally midship and away from elevators.
  • Seasickness: midship on decks 5 to 6, with a window or balcony if you can, so you can see the horizon.
  • Families: a midship cabin on a quiet deck, close to a bank of elevators.
  • Views: an outward-facing balcony on a quiet deck. Avoid any category the line labels as partial or obstructed view.
  • Budget: an interior cabin on a quiet deck gives the best sleep for the money; the deck matters more than the category for noise.

Star Princess tips

Cabana mini-suites on deck 9

Reserve Collection Cabana Mini-Suites (C1, C2) are on deck 9, the Promenade deck, which also has the lobby and Lido Marketplace, so it is busier than the decks above.

Decks 11, 12 and 14 are the quiet core

Decks 11, 12 and 14 have cabin decks directly above and below, the safest bet for light sleepers. Deck 10 is one floor above the busy Promenade deck, and deck 15 sits under the Sea View Terrace area on deck 16.

Top cabin decks meet the Dome

Deck 17 has cabins plus the Dome and Lido pool area, and deck 18 above it has the Sanctuary and fitness area. Light sleepers should go lower.

Low decks share space

Deck 6 mixes cabins with the spa and dining, and sits under the lounges on deck 7.

Cabins to think twice about

  • Deck 16 sits directly below Dome, Sundeck, Lido, Pools. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 17 sits directly below Dome, Sundeck, Sanctuary, Pool. Expect deck chairs, set-up and activity overhead from early morning.
  • Deck 9 sits directly above Dining, Shops, Casino, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 10 sits directly above Promenade, Lobby, Lido Marketplace, which can stay busy late.
  • Deck 5 shares the deck with Spa. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Deck 6 shares the deck with Spa, Dining. Cabins at the other end of the deck are usually quieter; check where the venue is.
  • Lowest forward cabins are closest to the bow thrusters and anchor gear, which can be loud when the ship docks or anchors early in the morning.
  • Cabins next to service areas, elevators or stair landings get more foot traffic and door noise. On the deck plan, look for unlabelled blocks (crew and service rooms) beside a cabin.
  • Partial or obstructed view categories usually mean lifeboats or structure in the way. The line labels these on its deck plan, so read the category name before you pay for a view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best deck to stay on Star Princess?

For a quiet night, pick a cabin on decks 11 to 12, 14, which have cabin decks directly above and below. Deck 12 is in the middle of that quiet block. Midship cabins move least in rough seas.

Which decks should I avoid on Star Princess?

Avoid decks 16 to 17 if you dislike noise overhead, because they sit directly under pool, sports or buffet areas. Light sleepers should also think twice about decks 9 to 10, which sit above bars, shops or entertainment venues.

Where is the best cabin on Star Princess for seasickness?

Book a midship cabin on the lowest cabin decks (decks 5 to 6). The CDC advises avoiding the parts of a ship with the most motion, such as the bow, and getting a view of the horizon helps, so an oceanview or balcony cabin low and midship is the ideal combination.

How many decks have cabins on Star Princess?

10 decks have passenger cabins: 5 to 6, 9 to 12, 14 to 17, based on the deck-by-deck listing of the current deck plan. Check the line's official deck plan for the exact cabin numbers on each deck.

Is a balcony worth it on Star Princess?

A balcony helps most on scenic or warm-weather itineraries and for anyone prone to seasickness, because a horizon view reduces symptoms. Check the category name first: inward-facing, promenade-view and partial-view balconies cost less but do not give a clear sea view.
